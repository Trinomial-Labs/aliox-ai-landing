Questions

When can I install it?

Soon. A pilot is running with a few stores now; the plugin and the plans open after it. Join the launch list and we’ll email you when they do.

Which stores does it work with?

WooCommerce stores on WordPress. The plugin checks that both are ready before it connects, then keeps your products, variations and policy pages in sync.

Does it take payment in the chat?

No. It puts the exact variation in the shopper’s own WooCommerce cart, and “added” only shows once WooCommerce confirms it. Checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce.

Will it make things up?

It answers only from your catalogue and the policy pages you approve, links to its source, and says plainly when nothing matches. Every reply is footed “AI assistant · Responses may be imperfect”.

What happens to shoppers’ details?

Shoppers stay anonymous. Emails, phone and card numbers they type are hidden, and each store’s data is kept separate from every other store’s.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language the shopper writes in.

What can’t it do yet?

Bundles, subscriptions, gift cards, add-ons, order lookup, refunds and payment in the chat are not supported. When a shopper asks, it says so and points them to your store.

What if Aliox is down?

Your store carries on. Browsing, cart and checkout never depend on the chat.

What happens at the monthly limit?

New chats pause until the next period and the store keeps selling as usual. You can change plans any time.
