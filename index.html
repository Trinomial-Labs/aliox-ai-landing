How it works

Live on your store in an afternoon

No new storefront and no code. Aliox AI plugs into the WooCommerce store you already run.

  1. Install the plugin

    Add Aliox AI to WordPress like any other plugin. It checks that your site and WooCommerce are ready.

  2. Connect your store

    Pair the plugin with your Aliox AI account. Your products, variations and policies sync, and stay in sync as you edit them.

  3. Make it yours and switch it on

    Choose the name, greeting, tone and colour, preview it in wp-admin, then turn it on for your shoppers.

On every page

There when shoppers need it, out of the way when they don't

The assistant waits as a small button in the corner of your store. A short nudge, in your own words, invites shoppers in, and one tap opens questions they can ask straight away.

  • Stays closed until a shopper wants help
  • Greets them with your nudge, like "Looking for something?"
  • Opens to suggested questions shoppers can tap
  • Full screen and thumb-friendly on phones

What it does

Everything a good shop assistant does, on every page, at any hour

Answers from your real catalogue

Shoppers ask in their own words, by colour, size, fit, material, price or occasion. They get real products from your store, and a plain answer when nothing matches.

The exact variation, in their own cart

The shopper picks the size, colour and quantity. Stock is checked again, and WooCommerce adds it to the cart they already have. Success shows only when the cart confirms it.

Policy answers with the source

Shipping, returns, exchanges and size guides come from the pages you approve, with a link to the source. If a policy is missing, it sends shoppers to you.

Looks like your store

Your colour, assistant name, greeting, avatar and placement, in light or dark. It feels like part of your shop, not a bolt-on.

Speaks your shopper's language

It replies in the language the shopper writes in, so more of your visitors get help that makes sense to them.

See what it sells

Your dashboard shows conversations, the products shoppers looked at, cart adds and checkout clicks, so you know what the assistant brings in.

Built on trust

An assistant your shoppers can believe

A sales assistant is only worth having if every answer holds up at checkout. Aliox AI follows four rules.

  • It never makes things up

    Prices, stock, products and policies come from your store. When your store doesn't say, the assistant says so.

  • The shopper stays in control

    Only the shopper adds items to their cart. Checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce, exactly as they are today.

  • Your store's data stays yours

    Every store is kept separate: its products, conversations and settings are never shared with another store.

  • Your store keeps selling

    If the assistant is ever unavailable, browsing, cart and checkout carry on as normal.
