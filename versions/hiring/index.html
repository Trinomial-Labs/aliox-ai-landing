  • Skills

  • Knows the whole shelf

    Searches your store’s real catalogue by colour, size, fit, material, price and occasion. When nothing matches, it says so plainly.

  • Puts the right size in the cart

    Re-checks stock for the exact variation, adds it to the shopper’s own WooCommerce cart, and shows “added” only once WooCommerce confirms.

  • Answers policy, with the page

    Shipping, returns, exchanges, payments, size guide and product care, from pages you approve, with a link to the source. A missing policy sends the shopper to you.

  • Speaks the shopper’s language

    Replies in the language the shopper writes in.

  • Calls the manager when asked

    Shoppers see “Talk to a person” only while someone on your team is set to Available. If nobody picks up within 3 minutes, it carries on and shows your contact link.

  • Greets from the corner

    A corner button with a nudge of up to 60 characters and up to four suggested questions. It opens full screen on phones.

  • Reports every shift

    Conversations started, product pages opened, added to cart, checkout clicks (never counted as orders), full transcripts and the searches that found nothing.
