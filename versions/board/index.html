Questions

What store owners ask us first, answered in full.

Will it make things up?

No. It answers only from your catalogue and the policy pages you approve, and says plainly when nothing matches. Policy answers carry a link to the page. If a policy is missing, it sends the shopper to you instead of guessing.

What happens at checkout?

Nothing new. Aliox adds the exact variation to the shopper’s own WooCommerce cart after re-checking stock, and shows “added” only once WooCommerce confirms. Checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the shopper’s language. In the pilot that is English and Bangla, and Banglish is being tested.

Can a person from my team take over?

Yes, if you turn on live chat (it is off by default). Live chats show in wp-admin under Aliox AI → Conversations. A shop manager takes one over and replies under their own name, always labelled as staff, then hands it back. Shoppers see “Talk to a person” only while a manager is set to Available; if nobody picks up within 3 minutes, the assistant carries on and shows your contact link.

What about shoppers’ privacy?

Shoppers stay anonymous. Emails, phone numbers and card numbers they type are hidden, and each store’s data is kept separate. You see full transcripts and the searches that found nothing.

What if Aliox is down?

Your store carries on as usual: browsing, cart and checkout all keep working without it.

What can’t it do yet?

Bundles, subscriptions, gift cards, add-ons, order lookup, refunds and payment in the chat are not supported.
