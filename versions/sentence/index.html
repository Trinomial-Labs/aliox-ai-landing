Questions

Will it work with my theme?

It’s an ordinary WordPress plugin and a chat widget on your pages. Simple and variable products are supported today; bundles, subscriptions, gift cards and add-ons aren’t yet, and we don’t promise every theme or plugin.

Can it take orders or payment?

No. It adds the exact variation to the shopper’s own cart, and checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce. It can’t look up orders or issue refunds, and it never reports a checkout click as a sale.

What does it know about my policies?

Only the pages you approve: shipping, returns, exchanges, payments, the size guide, product care. Each answer links to its source. If a policy is missing, the shopper is sent to you instead of a guess.

How does hand-over to my team work?

Turn on live chat for your store; it starts off. Shoppers see “Talk to a person” only while a manager is marked Available. Your team replies from wp-admin under their own names, and if nobody picks up within three minutes, Aliox carries on.

What happens at my monthly limit?

New chats pause until the next period. Browsing, cart and checkout carry on as before, so your store never stops. You can move to a bigger plan at any time.

Where do shoppers’ details go?

Shoppers stay anonymous. Emails, phone and card numbers they type are hidden from transcripts, and each store’s data is kept separate from every other store’s.
