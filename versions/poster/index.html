what does it do?

  • Shoppers ask in their own words: colour, size, fit, material, price, occasion. Aliox searches your real catalogue and says plainly when nothing matches.

  • It reads your variations and your stock, so when it says “Black / M is available”, it is.

  • The exact variation goes into the shopper's own WooCommerce cart. Stock is checked again, and “Added” only shows once WooCommerce confirms it. Checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce.

  • Shipping, returns, exchanges, payments, the size guide and product care, answered from the pages you approve, with a link to the page. If a policy is missing, it sends the shopper to you.

  • It replies in the shopper's language. The pilot runs in English and Bangla, and Banglish is being tested.

When can I get it?

Soon. A pilot is running with a few stores now; the plugin and the plans open after it. Join the launch list and we'll write when it does.

Will it make things up?

No. It answers from your catalogue and from the pages you approve, and when it doesn't know, it says so and points the shopper to your store. Every reply carries the line “AI assistant · Responses may be imperfect”.

Does it take payments?

No. It puts the exact variation in the shopper's own cart. Checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce, the way they are today.

What can't it do yet?

Bundles, subscriptions, gift cards, add-ons, order lookup, refunds and payment in the chat are not supported.

Can my team answer chats themselves?

Yes, if you switch live chat on (it's a per-store setting, off by default). Shoppers see “Talk to a person” only while a manager is set to Available. If nobody picks up within 3 minutes, Aliox carries on and shows your contact link.

What happens at my monthly limit?

New chats pause until the next period starts. Your store keeps selling as usual, and you can change plans any time.

What about shoppers' privacy?

Shoppers stay anonymous: emails, phone and card numbers they type are hidden. Each store's data is kept separate.

What if Aliox is down?

Your store carries on without it. Browsing, the cart and checkout still work.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the shopper's language. The pilot runs in English and Bangla, and Banglish is being tested.
