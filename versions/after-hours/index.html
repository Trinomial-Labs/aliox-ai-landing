Questions

Does it make things up?

No. It answers from your catalogue and the policy pages you approve. When nothing matches, it says so; when a policy is missing, it sends the shopper to you. Every reply is marked “AI assistant · Responses may be imperfect”.

What if Aliox is down?

Your store carries on. Browsing, the cart and checkout never depend on it.

What does it keep about shoppers?

Shoppers stay anonymous: emails, phone and card numbers they type are hidden. Each store’s data is kept separate.

What can’t it do yet?

Bundles, subscriptions, gift cards, add-ons, order lookup, refunds and payment in the chat. Checkout and payment stay in WooCommerce.

Which languages does it speak?

The shopper’s. It answers in whatever language the question was asked in.

Can my team answer instead?

Yes, if you turn on live chat for your store. A shop manager takes a chat in wp-admin, replies under their own name, and hands it back when done.

When can I get it?

A pilot is running with a few stores now. The plugin and plans open after it. Join the launch list and we’ll write when they do.
